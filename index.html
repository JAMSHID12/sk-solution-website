About Us

Local advisors, transparent process, real claim support

SK Solutions was established on August 15, 2019 in Kozhikode, Kerala, with a simple goal: help families and small businesses choose insurance they actually understand.

Our dedicated insurance agents and brokerage consultants are committed to transparent policies, honest premium comparisons across insurers, and dedicated end-to-end claim settlement support — from paperwork to payout.

We don't push a single insurer's products. Every recommendation starts with your situation — your health, your assets, your budget — and works backward to the right cover.
